About KamaAgni

A private playground for consenting adult couples.

How to use it

Open it together, set your names and heat level (Sweet, Spicy, Wild), pick a game, and play. Anything you don't both want — pass. Agree on a safe word first. Install it from your browser menu ("Add to Home Screen") to use it offline.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no servers. Your names, favourites, journal and settings live only in this browser on this device. Use ⚙️ → Backup to move them. Set a PIN in settings and use the 🕶 quick-hide button if someone walks in.

Consent

Adults only (18+). Everything is optional. A "no" or "pass" is always the right answer. Stop any time. Aftercare matters.
